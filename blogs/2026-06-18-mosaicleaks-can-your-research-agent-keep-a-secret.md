MosaicLeaks studies how deep research agents can leak sensitive enterprise information through the mosaic effect, where individually benign web queries combine to reveal private data. The authors propose PA-DR (Privacy-Aware Deep Research), a training method that improves strict chain success from 48.7% to 58.7% while reducing answer/full-information leakage from 34.0% to 9.9%, using a benchmark of 1,001 multi-hop research chains. A key finding is that privacy must be trained in rather than prompted in.
