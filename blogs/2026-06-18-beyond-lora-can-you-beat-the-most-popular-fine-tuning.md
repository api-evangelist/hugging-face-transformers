This post challenges the dominance of LoRA in parameter-efficient fine-tuning, noting it is used in 98.4% of PEFT model cards on the Hugging Face Hub. Benchmark evidence shows other PEFT methods such as OFT can beat LoRA on one or multiple axes of performance-to-memory tradeoff, and switching between PEFT techniques is as easy as changing one config in your code.
