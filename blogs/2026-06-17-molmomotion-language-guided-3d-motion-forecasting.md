MolmoMotion is a system that predicts future 3D object trajectories rather than only explaining motion that has already happened. It forecasts upcoming motion from video frames, marked 3D points, and action descriptions, addressing the needs of applications like robotics and video generation that must anticipate physical movement before it occurs.
